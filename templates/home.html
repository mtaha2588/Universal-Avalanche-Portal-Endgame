Pakistan's boundary is shown as per Pakistan's official position. Jammu & Kashmir is disputed territory; its final status is to be decided in line with the relevant UN Security Council resolutions. The Line of Control is shown dashed, and so is the part of the official outline that bounds India-administered territory: it is not drawn as a settled border. The study area is the Pakistan-administered area (all provinces and territories, including Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir). Glacier outlines are RGI v7.0 clipped to that area; glaciers elsewhere, including Siachen, are available as grey context. Glacier systems are traced from the RGI outlines (merged within about 5 km) and clipped to the official RGI v7.0 / GTN-G (2023) region limits, so the three systems never overlap. Sources: Natural Earth 1:10m; GTN-G 2023; RGI v7.0 (NSIDC). Elevation: Mapbox Terrain-DEM or AWS Terrain Tiles.
